The Starbreaker is forged from an ancient celestial tree and crown with items fallen from heaven. [[Thadius Quailwhistle]] could channel energy from the planes and even from distant stars.
It is among the most highly regarded constructions among the [[Argentum Arcanum, or the Silver Circle]] and considered to be among the most powerful.

[[The Starbreaker]] seems to be a key to the [[Ebonstar Palace]]. Without it's presence, the [[Ebonstar Palace]] shrinks. Could act as a key to the undergrounds of the palace.
