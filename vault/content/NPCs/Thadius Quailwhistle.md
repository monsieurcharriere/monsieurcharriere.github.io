### Thadius Quailwhistle

A powerful wizard. To the Drow, he is an unlikely ally. He is the enemy of the cult of [[Xhitigal, evil cruel wicked red dragon]]. Belongs to the [[Argentum Arcanum, or the Silver Circle]].

Members are known for their unusual magical specialties. [[Thadius Quailwhistle]] was one of the greatest members.

[[Thadius Quailwhistle]] and the [[Blackthorn Blades]] have become allies to combat the expansion of the cultists of [[Xhitigal, evil cruel wicked red dragon]].
He constructed the [[Ebonstar Palace]]. Corridors change through extradimensional spaces. The original temple below remains sealed. Is protected magically and has magical safeguards.

Thadius watched over the ruins for decades, then one day simply dissapeared.
Spellbooks remained open, no signs of struggle.
His magical staff [[The Starbreaker]] has gone missing. It has silver and mithril fittings and is the key to the deepest magical protections below [[Ebonstar Palace]].

3 things have gone missing:

- Himself, Thadius Quailwhistle
- [[Veyra Nightfang]]
- [[The Starbreaker]]
