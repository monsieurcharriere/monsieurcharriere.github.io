Airbike Workouts

1. Maximal Aerobic Speed (MAS)

3 km time trial

  • 3 km time trial
  • Do this first: all the other workouts are based on it
  • A good time? Aim for under 5 min
  • Average RPM: about 63-64 to get under 5 min
  • Use the link above (% RPM) to find the RPMs for the follow-up workouts

2. 10-20

Duration: ~15 min · 2 to 3 sets

  • 10 s hard, 20 s easy
  • Sets of 8 reps (4 min per set)
  • Pace: 120% of the average RPM from the 3 km test (e.g. 60 RPM average = sprint at 72 RPM)
  • 2 to 3 sets, total time: ~15 min

3. Three Periods

Duration: ~14 min · Great for hockey players

  • 1. 20 s / 10 s × 8, rest 1 min
  • 2. 30 s / 30 s × 4, rest 1 min
  • 3. 10 s / 20 s × 8
  • First two sets at similar speeds (110% of MAS), last one at 120% of MAS

4. Descending Ladder

Duration: ~10-12 min · Intensity: high

  • Distances: 1 km, 0.8 km, 0.65 km, 0.5 km, 0.3 km, 0.15 km
  • 1 min rest between each distance
  • Hold 110% of MAS on every interval
  • E.g. MAS of 64 RPM = aim for over 70 RPM

5. Calories EMOM

Duration: 10 to 20 rounds · Intensity: high

  • Every minute: 6 to 15 calories
  • Rest = remaining time in the minute
  • Adjust the target so you can complete all rounds

6. Pyramid

Duration: 25 min · Intensity: high

  • Efforts: 30 s, 60 s, 90 s, 120 s, 90 s, 60 s, 30 s
  • Rest equal to the effort duration
  • Steady but controlled pace

7. Zone 2 + sprints

Duration: 30 min · Intensity: moderate

  • 30 min at conversational pace
  • Every 5 min: 10 s sprint
  • Ideal for building an aerobic base

8. Tabata

Duration: 4 min · Intensity: maximal

  • 20 s all-out effort
  • 10 s rest
  • 8 rounds, 2 to 3 blocks with 4 min rest

9. Workout Finisher

Duration: ~5 min · Intensity: maximal

  • 3 all-out 15 s sprints
  • 2 min rest between each sprint
  • Go for maximum wattage
  • Try to maintain max wattage through all 3 trials
